04 Trailer · show the core loop

04 · Show gameplay immediately

Put a gameplay trailer first. Viewers may skip or scrub through it to judge the genre, art and whether the play appeals to them. Open with the core loop: a player action and its result, without a logo, cinematic or lore intro.

Use clear shots of about 3–5 seconds that make sense when sampled. Keep useful UI visible and action readable with sound off; add variety that shows more of the play.

Trailer advice: Derek Lieu. Gameplay-first guidance: Valve.

01 · Make the capsule readable

Choose one clear subject and a title that reads at thumbnail size. Adjust the composition for each capsule shape so the art still suggests the game’s setting or play.

Advice: Matt Hackett. Official asset guidance: Valve.

02 Short description · explain the hook

02 · Explain the hook quickly

Name the genre, describe what the player does, and give one distinctive reason to care. Use concrete action words; save background lore and detailed features for later.

Advice: Matt Hackett. Description guidance: Valve.

06 Tags · describe the play

06 · Choose tags that fit

Start with accurate, specific genre and gameplay tags. Order them by relevance to the experience; a popular tag is only useful if it describes your game.

Compare the tags of games closest to yours, and review the similarly tagged games in Steam’s tag wizard. Choose specific tags that accurately describe your game. Tags help inform More Like This and other recommendations; they do not guarantee placement.

Tag research advice: Chris Zukowski (How To Market A Game).

Official discovery and tag guidance: Valve.

About this game

Follow the signal

Start at a glowing source and trace its route through the garden. Each connection gives the signal a way forward; an unlit node shows where the route still needs work. Look along the whole path before choosing your next move.

Turn and branch

Rotate a junction to change where the signal travels. A branching connection can carry it towards more than one node, so a useful turn in one part of the garden may change another route. Try a connection, follow both branches and adjust the junctions together.

Connect the garden

Bring the separate routes into one connected garden. Follow each branch back to its source and look for the missing connection. The finished pattern should show how every node belongs, turning a collection of small choices into a route you can read.

05 Long description · make details scannable

05 · Make the details easy to scan

Use short sections and descriptive headings to explain the experience. Describe features truthfully and specifically, so players can tell what they will actually be able to do. Use short animated gameplay previews alongside the text to show key actions and how features work. Keep each preview focused on one idea.

Official description guidance: Valve.
